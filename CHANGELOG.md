# Changelog

## 0.4.1
- In the layout editor the tile's placeholder says "Tap for the code", as the tile on the screen does.

## 0.4.0
- The code closes by itself after a time you choose: Close the code after, in the tile's options (60 seconds
  unless you change it; 0 keeps it open until Back or a tap).

## 0.3.3
- The plugin and its tile are called Wi-Fi QR Code.

## 0.3.2
- The card's title says what it is: "Wi-Fi Network: " before the network's name.

## 0.3.1
- A network name with an emoji shows without it on the tile and the card's title, instead of a box; the code keeps
  the whole name, so phones still join the right network.

## 0.3.0
- Hide any of the tile's three lines with its switch: Show the top line, the middle line or the bottom line.

## 0.2.0
- The tile shows three lines: Wi-Fi QR code, the network's name, and "Tap for the code", without the Wi-Fi icon.
- Each of the three lines can have your own words: Top line, Middle line and Bottom line in the tile's options.
- The code stays readable on small screens, and the written password is left out when the screen cannot show all its characters.

## 0.1.0
- First version: a tile with your Wi-Fi network that shows a QR code to join it when you tap it.
