# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- Hide any of the tile's three lines with its switch: Show the top line, the middle line or the bottom line.

## 0.2.0
- The tile shows three lines: Wi-Fi QR code, the network's name, and "Tap for the code", without the Wi-Fi icon.
- Each of the three lines can have your own words: Top line, Middle line and Bottom line in the tile's options.
- The code stays readable on small screens, and the written password is left out when the screen cannot show all its characters.

## 0.1.0
- First version: a tile with your Wi-Fi network that shows a QR code to join it when you tap it.
